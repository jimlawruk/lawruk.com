Title: French Toast
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- bread slices | 8 | count
- eggs | 4 | count
- milk | 1 | cup
- vanilla extract | 1 | tsp
- cinnamon | 1 | tsp
- sugar | 1 | tbsp
- butter | 2 | tbsp
- salt | 0.25 | tsp

Steps:
- Prepare custard | In a wide bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, vanilla extract, cinnamon, sugar, and salt until fully blended.
- Dip bread | Heat a skillet or griddle over medium heat and melt 1 tablespoon of butter. Dip each bread slice into the custard, coating both sides, and let excess drip off.
- Cook slices | Place the soaked bread on the hot skillet and cook until golden brown on one side, about 2 to 3 minutes. Flip and cook the other side until golden.
- Repeat | Add more butter to the skillet as needed and continue cooking the remaining slices.
- Serve | Serve warm with syrup, powdered sugar, fruit, or your preferred toppings.
