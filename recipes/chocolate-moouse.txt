Title: Chocolate Mousse
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- dark chocolate | 200 | g
- eggs | 3 | count
- sugar | 2 | tbsp
- heavy cream | 1 | cup
- vanilla extract | 1 | tsp
- salt | 1 | pinch

Steps:
- Melt the chocolate | Break the chocolate into pieces and melt it gently in short microwave bursts or over a double boiler. Let it cool slightly.
- Whip the cream | Beat the heavy cream until soft peaks form, then set it aside.
- Beat the egg whites | Add the salt to the egg whites, whip to soft peaks, then gradually add the sugar and continue beating until glossy peaks form.
- Mix the yolks and chocolate | Stir the egg yolks into the melted chocolate after it has cooled enough to avoid scrambling them.
- Fold everything together | Fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture, then gently fold in the egg whites in batches to keep the mousse airy.
- Chill | Spoon the mousse into serving cups and refrigerate for at least 2 to 3 hours before serving.
