Title: Mashed Potatoes
Serves: 6

Ingredients:
- russet potatoes | 2 | lb
- butter | 4 | tbsp
- milk | 1 | cup
- salt | 1.5 | tsp
- black pepper | 0.5 | tsp
- garlic | 2 | cloves
- sour cream | 0.25 | cup

Steps:
- Prepare potatoes | Peel and cut the potatoes into even chunks. Place them in a large pot and cover with cold water.
- Boil | Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a simmer and cook until tender, about 15 to 20 minutes.
- Warm dairy | While the potatoes cook, heat the milk, butter, and minced garlic in a small saucepan until the butter is melted and the mixture is warm.
- Drain potatoes | Drain the potatoes well and return them to the pot.
- Mash | Mash the potatoes with a potato masher or ricer until mostly smooth.
- Add ingredients | Stir in the warm milk mixture, sour cream, salt, and black pepper until creamy. Adjust seasoning to taste.
- Serve | Transfer to a serving bowl and top with extra butter if desired.
