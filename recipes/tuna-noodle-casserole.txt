Title: Tuna Noodle Casserole
Serves: 6

Ingredients:
- egg noodles | 12 | oz
- canned tuna | 2 | cans
- condensed cream of mushroom soup | 2 | cans
- milk | 1 | cup
- frozen peas | 1 | cup
- onion | 1 | count
- celery | 1 | stalk
- shredded cheddar cheese | 1.5 | cup
- breadcrumbs | 0.75 | cup
- butter | 2 | tbsp
- salt | 1 | tsp
- black pepper | 0.5 | tsp

Steps:
- Preheat oven | Preheat the oven to 350 F and grease a 9x13-inch casserole dish.
- Cook noodles | Boil the egg noodles until just tender according to package directions, then drain and set aside.
- Sauté vegetables | In a skillet over medium heat, melt 1 tablespoon of butter and cook the chopped onion and celery until softened, about 4 to 5 minutes.
- Mix casserole | In a large bowl, combine the cooked noodles, drained tuna, cream of mushroom soup, milk, peas, sautéed vegetables, 1 cup of cheese, salt, and black pepper.
- Assemble | Pour the mixture into the prepared dish and top with breadcrumbs and remaining cheese. Dot the breadcrumbs with the remaining butter.
- Bake | Bake uncovered for 25 to 30 minutes, until bubbly and golden on top.
- Rest and serve | Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.
