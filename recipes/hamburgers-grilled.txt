Title: Hamburgers Grilled
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- ground beef | 1.5 | lb
- salt | 1 | tsp
- black pepper | 0.5 | tsp
- buns | 4 | count
- cheddar cheese slices | 4 | count
- lettuce | 4 | leaves

Steps:
- Preheat grill | Preheat the grill to medium-high heat and lightly oil the grates.
- Form patties | Divide the beef into 4 equal portions and shape each into a patty about 3/4 inch thick. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
- Grill patties | Place the patties on the grill and cook for 4 to 5 minutes per side for medium, or adjust time for desired doneness.
- Melt cheese | Add a slice of cheddar cheese to each patty during the final minute and close the grill lid until melted.
- Toast buns | Place the bun halves cut-side down on the grill for 30 seconds to toast lightly.
- Assemble burgers | Place lettuce on the bottom buns, then patties, and top buns.
- Serve | Serve immediately with your favorite sides.
