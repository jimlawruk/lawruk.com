Title: French Fries Air Fryer
Serves: 4

Ingredients:
- russet potatoes | 2 | lb
- olive oil | 2 | tbsp
- salt | 1 | tsp
- black pepper | 0.5 | tsp
- paprika | 1 | tsp
- garlic powder | 0.5 | tsp

Steps:
- Prep potatoes | Wash and peel the potatoes if desired, then cut into 1/3-inch sticks. Soak in cold water for at least 30 minutes to remove excess starch.
- Dry fries | Drain and pat the fries very dry with paper towels.
- Season | In a large bowl, toss the fries with olive oil, salt, black pepper, paprika, and garlic powder.
- Preheat air fryer | Preheat the air fryer to 380 F if your model requires preheating.
- Air fry | Arrange the fries in a single layer in the air fryer basket. Cook for 12 to 15 minutes, shaking the basket every 5 minutes for even browning.
- Crisp finish | If needed, cook an additional 2 to 4 minutes until golden and crispy.
- Serve | Remove from the air fryer and serve hot with ketchup or dipping sauce.
