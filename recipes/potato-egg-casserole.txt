Title: Potato Egg Casserole
Serves: 8

Ingredients:
- eggs | 12 | count
- milk | 2 | cup
- frozen hash browns | 4 | cup
- shredded cheese | 2 | cup
- breakfast sausage | 1 | lb
- onion | 1 | count
- bell pepper | 1 | count
- salt | 1 | tsp
- black pepper | 0.5 | tsp
- garlic powder | 1 | tsp
- paprika | 1 | tsp
- butter | 2 | tbsp

Steps:
- Preheat | Preheat the oven to 375 F and grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
- Cook meat | In a skillet over medium heat, cook the breakfast sausage until browned. Drain excess fat.
- Sauté vegetables | In the same pan, cook the onion and bell pepper until soft, about 3 to 5 minutes.
- Layer the base | Spread the hash browns evenly in the baking dish, then add the meat, vegetables, and about 1.5 cups of the cheese.
- Mix eggs | In a large bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
- Assemble | Pour the egg mixture evenly over everything, then top with the remaining cheese.
- Bake | Bake uncovered for 40 to 50 minutes, until the center is set and the edges are golden.
- Rest and serve | Let the casserole sit for 5 to 10 minutes before cutting into squares.
